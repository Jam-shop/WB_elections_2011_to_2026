## Insights

1. The political competition had suddenly reduced to just AITC and BJP in most constituencies in the game with either AITC or BJP winning in almost all constituencies in 2021. This was more clearly seen in the 2026 elections. 

2. Most (if not all) left wing parties like CPI, CPM, RSP, AIFB. SUCI etc. saw a decreased share in seats after AITC came in power and ultimately vanished from the completely from the 2021 elections.

3. While INC retained most of their seats from 2011 elections in 2016 elections too (or lost by a narrow margin). They also vanished 2021 onwards.

4. BJP established a strong foothold in West Bengal Legislative Assembly from the elections in 2021. They started garnering majority of their votes from the constituencies in North Bengal (a lot of which are SC and ST constituencies). 



Few things to note:
- In Murshidabad, AJUP appears to be one of the major competitors apart from AITC and BJP in 2026. AJUP was founded by Humayun Kabir who split from AITC about 5 months before the 2026 elections and also allegedly offered AITC a seat after the elections.
- GOJAM and GNLF are parties that contested for elections from Darjeeling. They campaign for the creation of a separate state Gorkhaland within India and garnered huge shares of votes (with GOJAM getting ~80% vote share in some constituencies). They too vanished starting from 2021 elections. GOJAM has been a part of the NDA alliance mostly, with a brief stint with TMC+ alliance. GNLF is also allied with the NDA. However a new party emerged, called BGPM, that is reportedly allied with AITC and also contested for elections in 2026.
